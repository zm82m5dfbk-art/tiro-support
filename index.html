Everything in one place

The support a club gives its players — in your pocket

Between team sessions, most players train without a plan. Tiro gives every session a purpose, and shows you whether it's working.

A plan that fits you

Your position, your level, how many days you can train, for how long and with what equipment — shaped around your matches, from pre-season to off-season.

A daily readiness brief

A 30-second morning check-in on sleep, energy, mood and soreness becomes a short brief on what to do today — with the research behind it.

Recovery that reads your body

A recovery score from your check-ins and Apple Health — sleep, HRV and resting heart rate. Mark what's sore and Tiro lightens or swaps the work.

A food tracker that's quick

Calories and macros at a glance. Log food in one tap or any amount in grams, scan a barcode, and keep an eye on water.

Progress you can see

Every workout saved, with a graph for any exercise you track. Plus top speed and distance from your watch or GPS vest, and tests to repeat every few weeks.

Reports for your coach

Export a clean PDF of your training, recovery, wellness and records to share with a coach, a physio or a scout.

Match Prep

Every day knows where it sits in your week

Tiro counts the days to and from each match — the way pro clubs plan a microcycle — and puts the right work on each one.

  • Field and gym sessions split across the week, two lifts in-season
  • Built in 4-week blocks for your position, level and the time you have
  • No heavy gym work within two days of a match
  • Feeling flat? Tiro can swap in a lighter session. Missed one? It finds space to catch up
  • Every suggestion can be switched off — it's a starting point, not an order

How it works

Set up in two minutes, then just play

No spreadsheets. Tell Tiro about your season once and it does the planning.

1

Tell Tiro about you

Your position, what you want to improve, how often you can train, and when your season and team trainings are.

2

Check in each morning

Thirty seconds on sleep, energy, mood and soreness. Connect Apple Health for even more.

3

Do today's work

Tick sessions off as you go, keep your streak alive, and watch your bests climb.

Private by design

Your data stays with you

Your training, health and food data live on your phone — not on our servers. We don't sell data and there are no advertising trackers.

On your device

Check-ins, sessions and meals are stored on your iPhone. Your account is only used to sign you in.

Apple Health, read-only

Tiro only reads what you allow, never writes to Health, and never uses health data for advertising.

You're in control

Export your data whenever you like, and delete your account from inside the app at any time.
