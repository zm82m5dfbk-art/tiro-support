Features

Everything your game needs, nothing it doesn't

A focused toolkit for footballers — training load, match performance and recovery in one place, designed to be used in the changing room, not at a desk.

Performance tracking

Log sprints, distance, minutes played and technical work. Watch your trend lines move week over week.

Training plans

Build a weekly schedule around fixtures, then tick sessions off as you go. Your plan adapts when life doesn't cooperate.

Recovery & wellness

Track sleep, soreness and readiness — including optional menstrual cycle tracking for athletes who want it.

Private by design

Secure sign-in, encrypted transport and no advertising trackers. Your data is yours — we never sell it.

Works offline

Log a session on a pitch with no signal. Everything syncs the moment you're back online.
